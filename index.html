Bivalve Field Guide & AI Morphometrics

Invasive Quagga Mussel (Dreissena bugensis)

Rapidly expanding deep-water biofouler. Displaces zebra mussels, filters out native phytoplankton, and colonizes soft muddy substrates.

AI ID: Asymmetrical fan outline, rounded ventral edge (cannot stand upright), faint pale concentric rings.

Established Zebra Mussel (Dreissena polymorpha)

Settled in Wisconsin lakes since the 1990s. Heavy biofouler on hard dock pilings and pipes. Removal yields 0 points (baseline established population) to teach target prioritization.

AI ID: Triangular D-shape, sharp lateral carina ridge, flat ventral side (stands upright on rocks), bold zig-zag stripes.

Native Freshwater Unionid (Endangered)

Vital ecological keystone filter-feeders that aerate lakebed sediment. Strictly protected: harming them triggers severe penalties (-300 PTS).

AI ID: Large elongated thick oval (2-3x dreissenid size), dark olive-brown shell, partially buried in sand with siphons exposed.

2. Multi-Modal Target Detection

  • eDNA Sonar Heatmap (Macro-tracking): The top-right minimap displays microscopic veliger larvae concentration (Blue → Yellow → Red plume). Audio sonar ping frequency accelerates as you approach live Quagga colonies!
  • Edge-AI Vision Reticles (Micro-targeting): On-device computer vision classifies bivalves in real-time with confidence metrics:
    [ ] QUAGGA (Green): Target invasive! Collect for +100 PTS. - - ZEBRA (Amber): Established Wisconsin baseline population (0 PTS). [X] NATIVE UNIONID (Red): Strictly endangered! Direct harm penalty: -300 PTS.

3. Eradication Tools & Energy Loop

  • Tool 1: Hydro-Vacuum: Concentrated suction cone (3.6m). Consumes 3.2% battery/s. Physical removal into onboard hopper—zero chemical runoff.
  • Tool 2: Chemical Micro-Sprayer: Targeted bio-pesticide cloud (3m radius). Kills clusters quickly, but lingering toxic cloud harms nearby native Unionids (-300 PTS) and fish (-150 PTS). Restricted in Sanctuary reefs!
  • Hopper Capacity (40 Mussels): When full (40/40), vacuum stops. Return to the glowing cyan DOCK STATION to empty your hopper and recharge battery.
  • Docking Station: Stand within the glowing ring to fast-recharge (9%/s) and bank all quaggas. Note: Unbanked quaggas held in hopper at mission end count at only 50% value!
